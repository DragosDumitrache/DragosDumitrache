## 🌱 What I care about

I like software that does two things at once: **solves a real problem**, and **leaves you feeling
something** while it does. Relief when the thing just works. A small grin at a well-placed detail.
The quiet trust you get from a tool that never surprises you in a bad way.

The good stuff usually comes from a few habits:

- 🧩 **Slim services, fat libraries.** Business logic lives in plain, unit-testable code, and services just wire it together.
- 🧊 **Immutable and stateless by default.** Fewer moving parts means fewer 3am surprises.
- 🧪 **Tests where they matter.** Unit tests for the logic, integration tests for the plumbing.
- ✂️ **Elegant over clever.** If it needs a diagram to understand, it probably needs a refactor.
- 💛 **Care about the last 10%.** Error messages, defaults, naming. That's where the feeling comes from.
